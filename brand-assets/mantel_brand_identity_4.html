04 — Signature Elements
The Heart Diacritic
The crimson open-stroke heart above the M is Mantel's signature mark — hand-drawn, not perfect. It signals warmth inside brutalism, the human gesture in a raw material world. Applied as a diacritic at every scale.

06 — Brand Voice
Warm, not soft.
Mantel speaks with the confidence of raw material — concrete, oak, iron — but tempered by the human warmth of a fireplace, a Persian rug, a handwritten name on linen. Minimal words. No fuss. Direct.
The space speaks first.
Copy is secondary to atmosphere. Short phrases, lowercase where intimate, uppercase where structural. Avoid adjectives that explain what the guest should feel — let the space do it.
